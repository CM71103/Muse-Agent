# Deck guidelines

These rules outrank your defaults for this project.

## One idea per slide
- A slide makes one claim. If you need a second one, add a slide.
- Headline ≤ 8 words, states the claim as a full sentence, never a label ("Results", not "Our Results Are Strong").
- No paragraph under a chart: say what the chart shows in one line, or put it in the speaker notes.

## Density
- ≤ 6 lines of body text on a slide, ≤ 12 words per line. Below that the audience reads instead of listening.
- A slide with a table keeps ≤ 5 rows and ≤ 4 columns. Larger goes to an appendix slide.
- Full-bleed images need one caption line; a decorative image needs none.

## Evidence
- Every number on a slide is traceable to the material that was given. No invented statistics, no aspirational charts.
- A chart without a legible axis label is not a chart: label the units and the period.

## Delivery
- Slide numbers except on the title slide. Progress must be visible at a glance.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` disables transitions, the overview grid still works.
- Print stylesheet: one slide per page, no clipping, no dark backgrounds bleeding through.

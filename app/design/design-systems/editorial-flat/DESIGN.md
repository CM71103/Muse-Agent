# Editorial flat

## Layout
- Single column, 72ch measure, generous 32–48 px gutters, no cards inside cards
- Rules (1 px `#dcd6cc`) instead of elevation; flat surfaces only

## Type
- Serif display (Georgia/Iowan stack) for headings, sans for UI chrome
- Scale 20/26/34/46 px for headings, 17 px body, line-height 1.6
- Small caps or 12 px tracked labels for section eyebrows

## Colour
- Paper `#f7f4ee`, ink `#211f1c`, one accent, deep teal `#155e63` or oxblood `#7b2d26`
- Tints at 8–12 % opacity for highlight blocks, never for text backgrounds below 4.5:1

## Components
- Tables: no zebra stripes, hairline row rules, right-aligned numerals
- Figures: caption below, numbered, `figcaption` in muted 13 px
- Deck slides: one idea per slide, headline ≤ 8 words, no body text under a chart
